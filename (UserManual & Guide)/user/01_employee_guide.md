# Employee guide

This guide is for **staff members**.  
It explains everyday tasks in simple steps.

---

## 1. Home dashboard

After you sign in, you land on **Home**.

Here you can usually:

- See today’s greeting and date
- See if you are clocked in, on leave, or done for the day
- Open shortcuts such as:
  - Leave
  - Claims
  - Payslips / payroll history
  - Announcements
  - Attendance history
  - Help

Pull down on the screen to **refresh**.

---

## 2. Clock in and clock out

Use the centre **Clock** tab on the bottom bar.

### Clock in

1. Tap **Clock**
2. Tap **Clock In**
3. Confirm
4. Wait until you see a success message

#### If workplace location is ON

Your company may require you to be at the office.

- Allow **location / GPS** when the phone asks
- Stay inside the allowed radius (example: 100 metres)
- The screen may show:
  - **In range**, or
  - How many metres away you are

If you are too far away, move closer and try again.

### Clock out

1. Tap **Clock**
2. Tap **Clock Out**
3. Confirm

Location is **not** required for clock out.

### Good to know

- You normally clock in **once per day**
- If you have **approved leave** for today, clock-in may be blocked
- You can view past records from attendance history / log shortcuts

---

## 3. Leave

### Apply for leave

1. From **Home**, open **Leave**
2. Start a new leave request
3. Choose:
   - Leave type
   - Start and end dates
   - Reason
4. Attach a file if needed  
   (example: medical certificate / MC for sick leave)
5. Submit

### After you submit

- Status is usually **pending** until a manager/admin reviews it
- You will see when it becomes **approved** or **rejected**
- Approved leave for today can block clock-in

---

## 4. Claims (expense claims)

Claims are in **Malaysian Ringgit (MYR / RM)** only.

### Submit a claim

1. From **Home**, open **Claims**
2. Start a new claim
3. Fill in:
   - Title
   - Category
   - Amount (RM)
   - Date of expense
   - Description if needed
4. Upload receipt(s) or supporting files
5. Submit

### Track your claim

Open **Claims** again to see whether it is:

- Pending
- Approved
- Rejected

---

## 5. Payslips

1. From **Home**, open payroll / payslips history
2. Tap a payslip to view details
3. Contact HR if something looks wrong

If the list is empty, HR may not have published a paid/approved payroll run yet.

---

## 6. Announcements

1. From **Home**, open **Announcements**
2. Read company posts
3. A badge may appear when there are unread items

---

## 7. Profile

Open the **Profile** tab.

You can usually:

- View / update personal details you are allowed to edit
- Save changes
- Open **Change password**
- Open **Help & support**
- Sign out

### Change password (while signed in)

1. Profile → **Change password**
2. Enter a new password and confirm it
3. Save

### Account email / username

These are set when you register.  
If you need them changed, contact HR or your system admin.

---

---

## 9. Daily checklist for employees

1. Arrive at work
2. Open myRekod → **Clock** → **Clock In** (allow location if asked)
3. Work as usual
4. Before leaving → **Clock Out**
5. Use Leave / Claims when needed
6. Check Announcements when a badge appears
