# Getting started

Welcome to **myRekod**.

This short page helps everyone open the app and understand the basics.

---

## What is myRekod?

myRekod helps your company manage:

- Clock in and clock out
- Leave requests
- Expense claims
- Payslips
- Company announcements

Employees use the staff screens.  
Administrators (HR / managers) use the admin dashboard.

---

## How to open the app

### Option A — Browser link

1. Open the website link your company gave you  
   (example: `https://attendance-app-peach-rho.vercel.app`)
2. Sign in with your account

### Option B — Add to Home Screen (recommended on iPhone)

1. Open the link in **Safari**
2. Tap **Share**
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**
4. Open myRekod from your home screen like a normal app

---

## Create an account (new users)

1. On the login screen, tap **Create an account**
2. Fill in:
   - **Email** — use an email you can open
   - **Username** — at least 3 characters (spaces OK, e.g. `AHMAD FAIZ`)
   - **Password** — at least 6 characters
   - **Confirm password**
3. Tap **Create account**
4. If asked to confirm email, check your inbox, then sign in

---

## Sign in

1. Enter your **username or email**
2. Enter your **password**
3. Tap **Sign in**

---

## Forgot password

1. On login, tap **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your account **email**
3. Tap **Send reset email**
4. Open the email and tap the link
5. Enter a **new password** twice and confirm
6. Sign in again with the new password

> Tip: Always request a **new** reset email if an old link expired.

# Part A — Employee guide

This guide is for **staff members**.  
It explains everyday tasks in simple steps.

---

## 1. Home dashboard

After you sign in, you land on **Home**.

Here you can usually:

- See today’s greeting and date
- See if you are clocked in, on leave, or done for the day
- Open shortcuts such as Leave, Claims, Payslips, Announcements, Attendance history, Help

Pull down on the screen to **refresh**.

---

## 2. Clock in and clock out

Use the centre **Clock** tab on the bottom bar.

### Clock in

1. Tap **Clock**
2. Tap **Clock In**
3. Confirm
4. Wait until you see a success message

#### If workplace location is ON

- Allow **location / GPS** when the phone asks
- Stay inside the allowed radius (example: 100 metres)
- The screen may show **In range**, or how many metres away you are
- If you are too far away, move closer and try again

### Clock out

1. Tap **Clock**
2. Tap **Clock Out**
3. Confirm

Location is **not** required for clock out.

### Good to know

- You normally clock in **once per day**
- If you have **approved leave** for today, clock-in may be blocked
- You can view past records from attendance history / log shortcuts

---

## 3. Leave

### Apply for leave

1. From **Home**, open **Leave**
2. Start a new leave request
3. Choose leave type, dates, and reason
4. Attach a file if needed (example: MC for sick leave)
5. Submit

### After you submit

- Status is usually **pending** until a manager/admin reviews it
- You will see when it becomes **approved** or **rejected**
- Approved leave for today can block clock-in

---

## 4. Claims (expense claims)

Claims are in **Malaysian Ringgit (MYR / RM)** only.

1. From **Home**, open **Claims**
2. Start a new claim
3. Fill title, category, amount (RM), date, description
4. Upload receipt(s) or supporting files
5. Submit
6. Track status: pending / approved / rejected

---

## 5. Payslips

1. From **Home**, open payroll / payslips history
2. Tap a payslip to view details
3. Contact HR if something looks wrong

If the list is empty, HR may not have published a paid/approved payroll run yet.

---

## 6. Announcements

1. From **Home**, open **Announcements**
2. Read company posts
3. A badge may appear when there are unread items

---

## 7. Profile

Open the **Profile** tab.

- View / update personal details you are allowed to edit
- **Change password** while signed in
- **Help & support**
- Sign out

Account email / username are set at registration. Contact HR to change them.

## 9. Daily checklist for employees

1. Arrive at work
2. Open myRekod → **Clock** → **Clock In** (allow location if asked)
3. Work as usual

# Part B — Admin guide

This guide is for **HR / administrators**.

---

## 1. Admin Home

After login (admin account), you see the **admin dashboard**.

### Today’s pulse

- Team size
- Clocked in
- Clocked out
- Items needing action (leave / claims)

### Shortcuts

| Shortcut | What you do there |
|----------|-------------------|
| Leave hub | Review leave requests |
| Payroll | Salaries, runs, payslips |
| Attendance | Who clocked in/out |
| Team | Employee list |
| Claims | Approve expense claims |
| Announcements | Post company news |
| Workplace location | Set office GPS + clock-in radius |
| Help & support | FAQs and support phone |

---

## 2. Employees (team)

1. Open **Employees** (or Team from Home)
2. Tap an employee
3. Edit details (name, role, phone, job, EPF, bank, etc.)
4. Tap **Save changes**

**Note:** Email and username are not edited on this screen.

---

## 3. Attendance

1. Open the **Attendance** tab
2. Review who clocked in / out today
3. Use calendar or monthly views for deeper checks

---

## 4. Leave management

1. Home → **Leave hub**
2. Open a request
3. **Approve** or **Reject** (add a reason when rejecting)

---

## 5. Claims management

1. Home → **Claims**
2. Check amount (MYR), category, receipts
3. Approve or reject

---

## 6. Payroll

1. Home → **Payroll**
2. Review salary settings and statutory settings
3. Create / open a payroll run
4. Review items → approve / mark paid
5. Employees then see payslips

---

## 7. Announcements

1. Home → **Announcements**
2. Create title and message → publish

---

## 8. Workplace location (geofence)

Sets **one office** for clock-in only. Clock-out is not checked. The pin does **not** follow the admin phone home.

1. Home → **Workplace location**
2. Enter workplace name
3. Set pin with **Pick on OpenStreetMap** or **Use my current location** (at the real office)
4. Set radius (example: 100 m; about 20–5000 allowed)
5. Turn **Enforce on clock-in** ON
6. Tap **Save workplace**

Tips: start with 100 m; increase only if GPS near the door often fails.

---

## 9. Admin daily checklist

1. Check Home pulse numbers
2. Review pending Leave and Claims
3. Check Attendance if needed
4. Post Announcements when needed
5. Keep Workplace location accurate
6. Run Payroll on schedule

# Troubleshooting

## Login and account

**Cannot create an account**  
Check email/password; try another username; if “too many attempts”, wait 15–60 minutes and try once.

**Email already exists**  
Sign in or use Forgot password.

**Forgot password**  
Send reset email → open newest link → set new password → sign in.

## Clock in / out

**Blocked by leave** — approved leave covers today.  
**Location required** — allow GPS and try again.  
**Outside workplace** — move closer, or ask admin to adjust pin/radius.  
**Already clocked in** — use Clock Out, or ask admin to check the record.

## Leave and claims

Pending is normal until admin reviews. Claims are **MYR only**.

## Payslips empty

HR may not have an approved/paid run yet.

## App feels slow

Close extra tabs; pull to refresh; prefer Add to Home Screen. For back navigation on web, use the app **←** button.
